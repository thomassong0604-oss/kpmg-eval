## Slide 4: Routing (1:15)

Now routing, with two corrections to what we said last week. First, the bars show coverage, not accuracy. Coverage means the right tools were called for a question. It does not mean the final answer was right. So 95 percent coverage is not 95 right answers out of 100. Second, the 95 percent belongs to calling every tool on every question. The keyword router on its own covers 83 percent. You also asked how we count unneeded calls. An unneeded call is a tool the question did not need. Total revenue needs finance data only, so if we call all three tools, two of those calls are unneeded. The table shows the trade-off. Calling every tool covers 95 percent but makes three calls per question, about two of them unneeded. The keyword router covers 83 percent with about one call. So coverage should always be read next to cost.
